Day 2 (8/9/26)
Today's goal is to transition from global scrambling to Block-Wise Chaotic Scrambling, the core foundation of learnable image encryption.
We achieve this by dividing the image into non-overlapping blocks of size $B \times B$ and permuting the blocks rather than individual pixels.
